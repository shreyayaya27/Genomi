Qura-Q 9-qubit VQC pilot (local Aer simulator only)

Scope: execution/pipeline verification only. These results are not meaningful estimates of clinical or generalization performance.
Samples: 233 (Tumor=120, Normal=113); target Normal=0, Tumor=1.
Features: NEK2, PKMYT1, MMP11, CPA1, COL10A1, HSD17B13, CA4, MYOC, LYVE1
Preprocessing: raw TPM -> log2(TPM + 1) -> StandardScaler.
StandardScaler was fit inside each training fold; no feature selection or dimensionality reduction was used.
Cross-validation: StratifiedGroupKFold(n_splits=5, shuffle=True, random_state=42), grouped by Case ID; first test fold has 24 Tumor and 23 Normal samples.
Quantum circuit: ZZFeatureMap(9 qubits, reps=1, linear entanglement) + RealAmplitudes(9 qubits, reps=1, linear entanglement).
Aer SamplerV2: 256 shots; COBYLA maxiter=20; no real hardware.
Circuit depth: 30; qubits: 9; feature-map parameters: 9; ansatz parameters: 18.

Out-of-fold accuracy: 0.442
Out-of-fold balanced accuracy: 0.443
Out-of-fold ROC-AUC: 0.477 (defined because both classes appear across out-of-fold predictions)
Confusion matrix: TN=53, FP=60, FN=70, TP=50
Total CV runtime: 37.32 seconds; mean fold training runtime: 7.25 seconds.

Limitation: this is still a small, non-independent pilot with a fixed optimizer budget; metrics must not be presented as clinical or generalization performance claims.
